# Tips and tricks

## Pasting
- **Paste a lot at once.** There is no need to do one match at a time; paste a whole stretch of history.
- **Overlap is safe.** Pasting the same matches again never double-counts them — repeats are matched by Session ID.
- **Paste each match once if it has no Session ID.** Matches without a readable ID can't be recognised as repeats.
- **Paste, Analyze, Clear, repeat.** The archive keeps every match you analyze, so you can clear the box between sessions without losing history.

## Getting better numbers
- **Add rules for your game modes.** Use the *Battle-type categories* section so Arcade, events or anything else gets its own row and its own averages for the goal calculator.
- **Pick the right battle type in the goal calculator.** The averages it loads come from the type you choose; "All Combined" mixes everything.
- **Play with "what if".** Edit the win rate or the average RP/SL in the goal calculator to see how a better win rate changes the number of matches needed.
- **Use the booster toggle** if you play with the +30% RP booster so the estimate matches reality.
- **RP vs "Other RP".** "RP by research target" is what goes toward new vehicles; module and research-progress RP is in the collapsed "Other RP" table. Both matter; they just aren't mixed.

## Advanced view
- **Hover amounts** in a match's detail to see the base, premium-account and booster parts of every SL and RP figure.
- **Use "Detail only"** to hide summary-only matches when you want vehicle or event analysis.
- **Open the insight tables you need.** Vehicle and event tables fill in when opened.
- **Sort by "Longest" or "Most SL"** to find your outliers quickly.
- **Export just one match** from its detail panel to share or save it.

## Backups
- **Export Raw before you clear browser data**, switch phones or reinstall your browser.
- **Prefer .json.gz** — it's the smallest, and the same data.
- **Check the match count** in the Advanced view's Storage section after a restore.

## Looks
- **Try Daylight** in bright sunlight or before printing.
- **Raise the text size** on a small screen instead of zooming the whole browser.
- **Move a setup between devices:** export your colour presets and import them on the other device.
- **Change two colours in a row** to go from a dark to a light look; the site waits until the pair is readable.

## Speed
- **Clear the paste box** after analyzing a very large log (the archive keeps the matches).
- **Use "Show all"** in the Basic table only when you need to scroll everything; the first 200 rows are usually enough.
- **Visit once while online**: after that, every later visit is instant and works offline ([Phone, offline and slow devices](phone-offline-and-slow-devices.md)).

## Keyboard
- **Esc** closes the tour, the Customise panel, the phone menu and the match-detail panel.
- **← →** move through the tour steps.
- Buttons, swatches and table rows can all be reached with **Tab** and activated with **Enter** or **Space**.
