# Accessibility

The site tries to work for as many people as possible, on any device. This page lists what is there and how to use it.

## Reading comfortably
- **Text size:** Small, Medium or Large in **Customise…**. Everything scales together.
- **Font style:** monospace, sans-serif or serif.
- **Colour:** eight presets, including the light **Daylight**, or choose any nine colours yourself.
- **Readability checks:** the panel measures the contrast of every text/background pair and **refuses** colour sets below 3:1; 4.5:1 or more is rated good. Text on buttons and banners flips between dark and light automatically.
- **Phone layout:** below about 680 pixels the top bar folds into a **Menu** with jump-to-section links.

## Keyboard
- Everything is reachable with **Tab**; swatches, buttons and library rows can be activated with **Enter** or **Space**.
- **Esc** closes whatever is on top: tour, Customise panel, phone menu, match detail.
- **← →** step through a guided tour.
- When a tour or the phone menu closes, focus returns to the control that opened it.

## Screen readers
- Swatches have names (Blue, Amber, …) and say whether they are pressed.
- The phone **Menu** button says whether it is expanded.
- Tours are dialogs with titles.
- Colour inputs carry labels such as *Background colour picker* and *Background as #rrggbb*.
- Preset buttons say what they do: *Use Crimson*, *Rename …*, *Delete …*.
- Status notes (update available, loading) are announced as status messages, and load failures as alerts.

## Motion
The tour's highlight animation and the menu's smooth scrolling are turned off when your device asks for **reduced motion**.

## Print
Printing uses a fixed readable size and your chosen colours; see [Basic view](basic-view.md#printing-and-save-as-pdf).

## Known gaps
- Hover tooltips (for example the SL/RP base/premium/booster breakdown) may not appear on touch screens; open the raw log text instead.
- Data tables are plain tables, with no row/column sorting by header.
- The documentation page follows your device's light/dark setting, not your site theme.

If something blocks you, please report it on the project's GitHub page — accessibility reports are welcome.
