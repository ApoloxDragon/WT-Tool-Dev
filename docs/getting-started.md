# Getting started

This page takes you from nothing to your first results in about two minutes.

## 1. Open the site
Open the hosted page in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari — desktop or phone). There is nothing to install and no account to make.

The first time you open each view, a short **guided tour** appears. It is skippable (**Skip tour**, or the **Esc** key) and can be reopened any time from **? Tutorial**. See [The guided tours](tutorials.md).

## 2. Get some log text
In War Thunder, open your battle results history and copy the text of one or more matches. Each match report starts with a line such as *Victory in the [Mode] … mission!* (or *Defeat…*) and ends with the *Session* and *Total* lines. You can copy many matches at once. [Reading your logs](reading-your-logs.md) shows what to look for.

No logs handy? Click **Load Example Data** to try the tool with a sample session.

## 3. Paste and Analyze
1. Click the big text box and paste.
2. Click **Analyze**.

The results appear under the box: overall totals, a split by battle type, the goal calculator, RP per research target and a table of every match.

## 4. What happens to your matches
Every match you analyze is also **saved, compressed, in your browser** (a small note under the buttons tells you how many are archived). That saved archive is what the [Advanced view](advanced-view.md) reads. It stays on this device, in this browser only.

**Important:** because the archive lives only in your browser, clearing your browser's site data deletes it. Use **Export Raw** now and then as a backup — see [Backups, import and export](backups-import-export.md).

## 5. Next steps
- Try the **goal calculator** to see how many matches a research goal needs: [The Basic view](basic-view.md#goal-calculator).
- Click **Advanced view →** to open a single match in detail.
- Pick a colour theme with the swatches in the top bar.
- Read [Tips and tricks](tips-and-tricks.md).
