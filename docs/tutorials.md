# The guided tours

The site has built-in tours that spotlight one control at a time and explain it. They are the quickest way to learn the page you are on.

## How tours work
- Each main view opens its tour **once, on your first visit**. After that, open it any time with **? Tutorial** in the top bar.
- Move with **Next** and **Back**, or the **← →** keys. **Skip tour** or **Esc** closes it.
- The card places itself next to the thing it explains (below, above or beside), and scrolls it into view. On a phone it opens the **Menu** first when a step is inside it.
- If a step points at something that is not on screen yet (for example results before you click Analyze), it tells you what will appear there.

## The Basic view tour
1. **Welcome** — what the site does and how the tour works.
2. **Paste your logs** — one or many matches; overlaps are fine.
3. **Analyze** — plus *Load Example Data* and *Clear*.
4. **Battle-type categories** — add your own keyword rules.
5. **Results** — overall totals and the split by battle type.
6. **Goal calculator** — matches needed for an RP or SL goal.
7. **Per-match detail** — duplicates are struck through.
8. **Export and import** — HTML, minimal, raw and import.
9. **Advanced view** — the saved archive and match detail.
10. **Colours** — presets and Customise.

## The Advanced view tour
1. **Welcome** — what the archive is.
2. **Filter and sort** — search, battle type, win/loss, sort, detail only.
3. **Open a match** — ● has detail, ○ is summary-only.
4. **Match detail** — breakdown, hover for SL/RP parts, copy, export, delete.
5. **Insights** — by map, vehicle and event type.
6. **Storage and backups** — sizes, raw export/import, duplicates, delete all.
7. **Colours** and **Back to Basic**.

## The colour panel tour (optional)
Open **Customise…** and choose **? How this works** at the bottom. It explains presets, what each of the nine colours changes, the readability check, font and size, saved presets and reset. It **never starts by itself**.

## Tips
- If you skipped a tour by accident, just reopen it.
- Tours are remembered per view in your browser. Clearing site data makes them appear again as if it were your first visit.
