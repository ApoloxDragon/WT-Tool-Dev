# Recipes: how do I…?

Short, practical answers. Each ends with where to read more.

## …see how my session went?
Paste the logs, click **Analyze**, read **Overall** and **Battle type split**. → [Getting started](getting-started.md)

## …find out how long until I unlock a vehicle?
Analyze some recent matches, open the **Goal calculator**, choose the battle type you play, type the **RP goal remaining** (what the game shows you still need) and read **matches needed** and **estimated playtime**. Toggle **+30% RP Booster** if you use one. → [The goal calculator in depth](goal-calculator-guide.md)

## …compare two game modes?
Add a rule for each mode in **Battle-type categories**, click **Analyze**, and compare their rows in the split table (win rate, average win/loss SL, total RP). → [Battle-type categories](categories-guide.md)

## …see which maps are bad for me?
Advanced view → **Insights → By map**. Judge maps you've played several times. → [Insights explained](insights-guide.md)

## …see which vehicle earns best?
Advanced view → **Insights → By vehicle** for time, play RP and kills; open individual matches to see per-vehicle activity. → [Reading a match's detail](match-detail-guide.md)

## …check one particular match?
Advanced view → search the mission or target, click the **●** row. Open the **raw log text** to verify any figure. → [Reading a match's detail](match-detail-guide.md)

## …find my best (or worst) matches?
Advanced view → **Sort** by *Most SL*, *Most RP* or *Longest*, optionally filter to *Victories* or *Defeats*.

## …find out what a premium account or booster added?
Open a match, expand an event section, and **hover an SL/RP amount** for the base + premium + booster parts.

## …avoid double-counting when I paste the same matches twice?
You don't need to do anything: repeats are recognised by Session ID and left out. If a match has no readable Session ID, paste it only once. → [Reading your logs](reading-your-logs.md#session-ids-and-duplicates)

## …keep a long history safely?
Analyze regularly (it saves automatically) and **Export Raw** now and then. → [Backups, import and export](backups-import-export.md)

## …move everything to a new phone or computer?
**Export Raw** on the old device, copy the file across, then **Import Report** (Basic) or **Import raw / log file** (Advanced) on the new one. Re-create your category rules, and export/import your colour presets if you made any. → [Where your data goes](data-lifecycle.md)

## …share my results with a friend?
Use **Export HTML** for a standalone, themed report of the current session (unique matches only). It contains your results, so share it only with people you want to see them. For the numbers only, use **Print / Save PDF**.

## …print or save a PDF?
Click **Analyze**, then **Print / Save PDF**, and choose *Save as PDF* as the destination. The printout keeps your theme and leaves out duplicates. → [The Basic view](basic-view.md#printing-and-save-as-pdf)

## …clean out repeated matches that slipped in?
Advanced view → **Storage → Clear duplicate matches**. It keeps the first copy of each.

## …delete just one match?
Advanced view → open it → **Delete from archive**.

## …start completely fresh?
Back up first (**Export Raw**), then Advanced view → **Delete entire archive**. In the Basic view also click **Clear**.

## …read it comfortably on my phone?
Use the **Menu** button for jump links, raise **Text size** in **Customise…**, and choose a high-contrast preset. → [Phone, offline and slow devices](phone-offline-and-slow-devices.md)

## …use it with no internet?
Visit once while online; after that the site works offline. → [Phone, offline and slow devices](phone-offline-and-slow-devices.md#offline-use)

## …make it match my taste?
Pick a swatch, or **Customise…** to change all nine colours, the font and text size, and save your own presets. → [Colours and text](colours-and-text.md)

## …restore after clearing my browser?
Import your latest raw export. Re-create category rules and re-import colour presets. → [Backups, import and export](backups-import-export.md)

## …check that my backup is current?
Advanced view → Storage: the note under the tiles says when you last exported and how many matches have been added since.

## …tell whether a match has detail?
In the Advanced view's library, **●** has detail and **○** is summary-only.

## …get a tour again?
**? Tutorial** in the top bar (or **? How this works** in the colour panel). → [The guided tours](tutorials.md)
