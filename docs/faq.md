# Frequently asked questions

Fixes for specific problems are in [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). This page answers the "why" and "what is" questions.

## About the site

**What is it for?** Turning War Thunder's per-match text reports into totals, averages, a research goal estimate and a searchable personal archive.

**Is it official?** No. It is an independent fan tool and has no connection with the game's publisher.

**Does it work with the game running?** It is not connected to the game at all. Copy text out of the game, paste it into the site.

**Is it free?** Yes. It is open source (see the project's licence on GitHub).

**What is the Dev version?** The in-progress copy of the site where new features are tried first. The stable version is the released one. They don't share archives.

**Which should I use?** Stable if you want the most reliable experience; Dev if you want newer features and don't mind occasional rough edges. Back up either with **Export Raw**.

**Is it written by AI?** Largely, yes — under the owner's guidance, review and corrections, and held to the same rules as any other code. See [Project rules](project-rules.md#ai-generated-content).

**Where does the sample data come from?** From the project owner, Apolox — their own matches. See [Project rules](project-rules.md#sample-data).

**Does the site send anything anywhere?** No. Everything is processed in your browser and nothing leaves your device unless you export a file yourself ([Project rules](project-rules.md)).

## About your data

**Where is my data?** Only in your browser on this device. See [Where your data goes](data-lifecycle.md).

**Does anyone see my matches?** No. Nothing is uploaded. See [Privacy and safety](privacy-and-safety.md).

**Why did I lose my archive?** Almost always because browser site data was cleared, a private window was used, or a different browser/device/address was opened. Restore from a raw export.

**Do I need an account?** No.

**How big can the archive get?** Matches compress to a small fraction of their size. The Advanced view shows your usage and your browser's allowance. Thousands of matches are fine on a normal device.

**Can I use it on two devices?** Yes, but they don't sync. Move data with a raw export and import.

## About the numbers

**Why is Total RP lower than the number at the end of the game's Total line?** The site counts CRP, not the larger combined figure. See [Reading your logs](reading-your-logs.md#why-crp-and-not-the-last-rp-figure).

**Why don't "RP by research target" and "Total RP" match?** They are different measures: one counts RP credited to targets, the other each match's CRP.

**Why are my duplicates struck through?** The same Session ID appeared more than once; only the first counts.

**Why does the goal calculator say SL-bound?** Your SL goal takes more matches than your RP goal. See [The goal calculator in depth](goal-calculator-guide.md).

**Why is my win rate different between battle types?** Because modes differ a lot. That is why the split exists.

**What does "net SL" mean?** SL earned minus costs. See [Key concepts](concepts.md).

## About the features

**What's the difference between Export HTML, Minimal and Raw?** HTML is a readable report, Minimal is a small summary file, Raw is the real backup with original text. See [Backups, import and export](backups-import-export.md).

**What do ● and ○ mean?** ● has stored detail; ○ is summary-only.

**Can I change the battle types?** Yes: [Battle-type categories](categories-guide.md).

**Can I use my own colours?** Yes, all nine, plus font and size: [Colours and text](colours-and-text.md).

**Why a Menu button on my phone?** The top bar is folded to fit; see [Phone, offline and slow devices](phone-offline-and-slow-devices.md).

**Does it work offline?** Yes, after one visit online.

**Can I print it?** Yes, with your theme; see [Basic view](basic-view.md#printing-and-save-as-pdf).

## About updates

**How do I know a new version is available?** A note appears: *A new version is ready.* with **Reload** and **Later**.

**Will an update delete my data?** No. Your archive and settings are separate from the site's files. (Keep a backup anyway.)

**Why do new features sometimes show up only after Reload?** The offline copy of the site updates in the background and waits so nothing changes under you while you work.
